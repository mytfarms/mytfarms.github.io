About Cade Christensen

Cade Christensen

Founder & Auctioneer

Certified Auctioneer

The Man Behind the Mic

A Lifetime in the Ring.
A Modern Approach to the Market.

I've spent my whole life around brick and mortar livestock auctions — and in today's world, online auction platforms handle a good deal of business too. I love a rapid and clear chant, and I work on perfecting it every single day. But there's a whole lot more to getting things sold in a customer-centric environment than the chant itself.

"Get it sold, get paid, and get on. No getting mired down in photo files, software, payment issues, or an ego maniac on the mic."

Marketing, website navigation, a track record of honest dealings, and the ability to take care of a customer you may never have the pleasure of shaking hands with — these are just facts of the modern world. But auctions, no matter how you prefer to conduct them, are still the best way to find the market and get top dollar for your asset. It's my job to find the right buyers, the right timeline, and the most hassle-free method for your particular assignment.

We handle the "sausage grinding" behind the scenes so you can enjoy an aromatic breakfast experience on the front end. Although my official certification is new, my life experience has been accumulating since my dad bid on, bought, calved, and re-sold those first "gummer" grandma cows with one calf in the chamber — back when I could barely walk. Everyone in that scenario found value, added their piece of the puzzle to increase their margin, and all parties walked away with the full picture, money in their pocket, and satisfaction with a trade well made.

That's the beauty of auction marketing. Everyone gets to win. My sellers get a hassle-free experience, and buyers get the confidence that they are getting exactly what is advertised. If trust is our commodity, then providing "aspirin" to the seller and buyer is the service. No headaches, no heart attacks — just smooth sailing to conduct business.

We value yours.
