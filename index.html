Common Questions

Got Questions?
We've Got Answers.

What types of auctions does MYT Auctions offer?

MYT Auctions offers live on-site auctions, online-only auctions, and simulcast auctions where bidders can participate both in person and online simultaneously — giving sellers maximum exposure and competitive pricing.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Colorado and the greater high plains area, bringing buyers and sellers together across the region with the reach to attract competitive bids wherever your assets are located.

Why should I sell through auction instead of privately?

Auctions promote true price discovery through fair and open competition, ensuring maximum value for sellers. There's no prolonged negotiation — you set a date, buyers compete, and you get paid. Maximum money, minimum headache.

How does MYT Auctions ensure a fair process for buyers?

Every auction we conduct is run as a public, fair, and straightforward forum. All bidders compete on equal terms, with transparent rules and no favoritism — building the trust and confidence that keeps strong buyers returning to our auctions.
